Plan on map
Add point, click a distinctive spot on the plan above (a gate, a corner), then the same spot on the map. Two points fix scale and rotation exactly; more points average out small mistakes. Zoom the map in for accuracy.
Check the plan against map features: roads, buildings, the scale bar at the bottom left. The red frame is the plan; its top edge is marked. Confirm only when the orientation and size look right.
Geotagged photos
Faded photos on the map come from their GPS tags. Tick the ones that belong on this plan. Only ticked photos are imported onto the plan, and only after you confirm the placement.
Layers
Areas is the base layer: its region names make the photo file names, and it cannot be renamed or deleted. A new layer's name becomes a tag. Regions you draw go into the layer shown here.
Visibility (base layer)
Untick a tag to hide the photos that carry it. Only the plan changes; the photo list still shows everything.
