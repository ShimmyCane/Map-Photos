# Sample project: Hollywood Bowl

A ready-made site plan (a satellite view of the Hollywood Bowl) with regions and markup already drawn. Add your own photos to try the whole workflow.

Contents:
- `hollywood_bowl_site_plan.pdf` the plan
- `hollywood_bowl.mapphotos.json` regions, notes, an arrow and a revision cloud
- `photos/` five sample photos (marquees, fountain, stage views). Add more of your own here (JPG, PNG or HEIC)

## How to use it

1. Open `index.html` (in the folder above) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click **Open JSON** and choose `sample-project/hollywood_bowl.mapphotos.json`.
3. A dialog asks for the files. Click **Choose folder**, pick the `sample-project` folder, then click **Open project**. The plan appears with six regions (Seating Bowl, Band Shell and Stage, Museum and Terrace, Picnic Area 6, Lot B, Highland Ave Entrance), the markup, and the five sample photos already placed. Skip to step 7 if you only want to try the markup tools, or delete the photos from the plan (double-click each) to practise placing them yourself.
4. To add more photos, put them in `sample-project/photos/`, click **Folder** under Photos in the right panel and choose that folder (or use **Load photos** to pick files). They sort by time taken. The sample photos carry made-up times (17:35 to 18:12) so they sort as a walk from the Highland Ave marquee to the seats.
5. Drag a photo from the list onto the plan where it was taken. The card shows the region it landed in. Hover a photo to enlarge it. Drag a placed photo to move it. Double-click it to take it off the plan.
6. Tick regions under **Filter by region** to show only those photos.

## Try the markup tools

The three tools are in the left rail under the region tools. Click the tool, then use the plan. Click the tool again to switch it off.

7. **Text note** (the T icon): click on the plan, type the note, press **Ctrl+Enter** (or Save). Enter adds a new line.
8. **Arrow** (the diagonal arrow icon): press at the tail, drag, and release at the point.
9. **Revision cloud** (the cloud icon): drag a rectangle around the area that changed.
10. Everything you add is listed under **Markup** in the right panel. Use **Edit** to change a note's text, **Delete** to remove an item, or **Clear all markup**.

## Save and export

11. **Save JSON** keeps regions, markup and photo positions (the photos themselves are not copied).
12. **Download PDF** writes a PDF with the regions, markup and placed photos on the plan.
13. **Download photos** saves the placed photos renamed by region (`Lot_B_01.jpg`, and so on).
